# Idea 05 disposition

**Status: insufficient_evidence; mechanism evidence retained.** The frozen five-epoch pilot compares the strict-inner pooled decoder feature with a separate adapter over raw CLIP/SlowFast temporal features, on one action and one composition fold with seed 3407. These are exploratory Novel-dev results; formal U remains untouched.

On A1_action_01, the pooled arm reached Novel AUROC 0.6707 and the raw-sequence arm 0.6335 (raw minus pooled -3.72 pp; paired video-cluster 95% CI -8.97 to +1.67 pp). Raw sequence substantially improved same-query cross-video PairAcc from 0.483 to 0.618 (paired query-video-component bootstrap CI for the difference +0.109 to +0.667), while cross-query AUC fell from 0.672 to 0.634. Same-video PairAcc fell from 0.727 to 0.643. This is evidence that the raw path can improve within-query compatibility while worsening cross-query score comparability; it does not improve the primary pooled metric on this fold. The query-only AUROC was 0.648. Fresh pre-fusion video replacement moved the raw arm from 0.634 to 0.592 with original labels retained as a dependence diagnostic.

On C1_composition_01, Novel AUROC rose from pooled 0.5529 to raw 0.5925 (+3.96 pp; paired video-cluster 95% CI -4.00 to +11.64 pp). Same-query PairAcc rose from 0.516 to 0.549, but its paired component CI (-0.353 to +0.050) includes zero; same-video PairAcc was unchanged at 0.671. Cross-query AUC rose from 0.554 to 0.593. Query-only AUROC was 0.572. The raw shuffled-video retained-label AUROC was 0.574 versus 0.592 on real inputs; this indicates limited video dependence in this diagnostic, not a valid relabeled benchmark.

Across these folds the raw-minus-pooled pooled AUROC mean is only +0.12 pp, with opposite fold effects and broad uncertainty. The screen therefore fails the frozen +2 pp/conditional-consistency criteria. Seen AUROC changed -0.75 pp on A1 and +3.41 pp on C1; frozen localization remained exactly the same within each fold. The raw arm has 75,649 parameters versus 33,665 for pooled, so the comparison is not parameter matched. It also covers only the Moment strict-inner backbone and one seed. No fully crossed counterfactual query-video matrix was verified; source-linked same-video positive/negative pairs were available and reported.

The main hypothesis that raw sequence features reliably restore pooled semantic-novel existence AUROC is not supported by this screen. The conditional result is a useful mechanism clue: on the action fold, stronger same-query ranking is offset by worse cross-query ordering. Do not promote this adapter as a solution. No extra method variant is justified before reviewing candidate-level evidence. **Next priority: Idea 06, evidence-aware MIL aggregation**, beginning with its candidate-score, mask, and negative-provenance gate. If no valid candidate path exists, record a specific blocker and proceed to 07.

## Frozen score-correction follow-up (2026-10-04)

This was a score-only follow-up, not a new training run; one seed and two folds remain exploratory. Each correction evaluation emitted all 960/1,145 expected Seen+Novel rows, reused the same 75,649-parameter adapter checkpoint, and took 6.25/6.05 seconds including fresh shuffled forwarding; no retraining or U access occurred.

This rejects the specific claim that subtracting the query-only score, with a coefficient selected on Seen validation, repairs Novel score comparability in these folds. It does not establish that query priors are the sole cause of the three baseline degradation: the correction is a one-seed, two-fold post-hoc screen, and there is no stable conditional gain to extend. Retain the earlier A1 same-query raw-sequence signal as a mechanism clue only. **Disposition remains `insufficient_evidence`; do not promote the adapter or correction.** No additional Idea05 configuration is justified. Across this bounded screen, the next useful step is independent evaluation across more held-out semantic folds/backbones only if a predeclared candidate emerges from new evidence; the current data do not nominate one. Official U remains unopened.
