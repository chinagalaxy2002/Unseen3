# Idea 04 disposition

**Status: insufficient_evidence.** This one-seed, two-fold strict-inner pilot evaluates whether query-marginal sampling improves semantic-novel existence AUROC and video-conditioned compatibility. Formal U remains untouched; inner Novel-dev results are exploratory.

The balanced-query arm M2 changed A1_action_01 pooled Novel AUROC from 0.6169 to 0.6472 (+3.03 pp; paired video-cluster 95% CI +0.32 to +5.88 pp), but Seen AUROC fell 1.72 pp. Its same-query PairAcc fell from 0.494 to 0.455, same-video PairAcc stayed 0.713, and the within-query AUROC fell while cross-query AUROC rose. Query-only AUROC was 0.671, higher than M2. The gain is therefore **prior-driven/cross-query calibration evidence**, not evidence that video-query compatibility improved. Pre-fusion shuffled-video re-forward retained-label AUROC fell from 0.647 to 0.622; this shows some video-input dependence but is only a diagnostic and does not overturn the conditional evidence.

On C1_composition_01, M2 changed pooled Novel AUROC from 0.5471 to 0.5498 (+0.28 pp; paired video-cluster 95% CI -0.36 to +1.05 pp), with Seen AUROC down 0.27 pp. Same-query PairAcc rose 0.019 and same-video PairAcc fell 0.013; both estimates are imprecise. Query-only AUROC was 0.583. The shuffled retained-label diagnostic moved from 0.550 to 0.534. This fold does not show a reliable pooled benefit.

The pilot tests query-marginal training, with compute-matched natural-duplicate and original-ratio controls. Evidence opposes the claim that balanced-query training reliably improves video-conditioned compatibility: the action fold's pooled gain tracks cross-query ranking and violates the Seen guard, while the composition fold is near null. One seed and two folds do not establish a general effect. Same-query query-only estimates are cache-sensitive because repeated rows for an exact text have slightly different cached embeddings; they are not interpreted as valid compatibility controls. Frozen localization was unchanged (R@1 IoU .5: 0.348 action, 0.350 composition); each arm has 50,049 parameters.

No extra hyperparameter revision is justified. **Next priority: Idea 05, independent compatibility adapter**, to test whether the representation path, rather than marginal sampling, limits conditional evidence. Preserve the query-prior result as a diagnostic and do not promote M2 as a candidate.
