## Gate decision

**Insufficient evidence; do not advance to DG.** Mean B1−B0 pooled Novel change is +0.86 pp, below the frozen +2 pp development reference, with wide video-cluster intervals crossing zero in both folds. A1 pooled AUROC is effectively unchanged while exact same-query PairAcc falls by 7.9 pp; C1 gains 1.72 pp pooled and 5.2 pp same-query PairAcc, but both paired intervals are wide and same-video source PairAcc is unchanged. The fold directions do not replicate. Query-only is a strong control on C1, and the pre-fusion shuffled-video diagnostic retains much of natural AUROC in both folds. No DG objective (MLDG/IRM/Group DRO) was trained because its prerequisite, reproducible conditional benefit from the same pair signal, was not met. This gate is one seed/two folds on one Moment raw-sequence adapter; it does not establish a three-baseline or cross-backbone result. Official U was not accessed.
