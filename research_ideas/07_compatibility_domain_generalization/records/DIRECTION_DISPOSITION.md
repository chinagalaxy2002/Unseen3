# Direction 07 disposition

**Disposition: insufficient evidence for domain-generalization optimization.** The training-side support audit found source-linked reviewed positive/negative units on the same video: 691 A1 units and 1,119 C1 units, each with human-temporally-annotated present query, user-attested full-query absent edit, and exact video/source linkage. The small gate compared a 75,649-parameter raw-sequence adapter with natural BCE plus matched source-pair replay (B0) against the same exposure with an additional fixed pair margin (B1); each arm used five epochs, one seed, Seen-only checkpoint selection, and identical pair replay/update counts. All four tasks passed pipeline artifact and prediction-coverage validation, including fresh raw-video shuffling before query/video interaction.

The pooled Novel AUROC deltas B1−B0 were +0.00 pp on A1 (paired video-cluster 95% interval [−4.68,+3.91]) and +1.72 pp on C1 ([-5.58,+8.64]), mean +0.86 pp. Exact same-query PairAcc changed 0.629→0.551 on A1 (paired connected-component interval [−0.571,−0.056]) and 0.512→0.563 on C1 ([-0.214,+0.227]); source-linked same-video PairAcc changed 0.734→0.706 on A1 (video interval [−0.120,+0.056]) and remained 0.645 on C1 ([-0.127,+0.128]). Thus pair-margin learning did not produce repeatable conditional compatibility gains. Query-only AUROC was 0.648 on A1 and 0.572 on C1; video-only was 0.506/0.514. The retained-label pre-fusion shuffle AUROCs were 0.634/0.647 on A1 and 0.579/0.564 on C1, close enough to the natural scores to prevent a strong video-dependence claim; these shuffles remain diagnostics, not relabeled test sets. Frozen localization was unchanged; Seen changes were −0.77 pp and +0.36 pp.

The round tested the gate that ordinary source-linked pair supervision can learn a transferable relation before investing in environment-aware optimization. A1 exact same-query ranking regressed, while C1 showed a small uncertain improvement; same-video ranking did not improve. The code executed and controls validated, so the limiting evidence is method/signal support, not missing pair labels or an execution failure. We did not run MLDG, IRM, Group DRO, additional seeds, or another backbone. No DG contribution is claimed.

Keep the completed environment manifest and all fold-level predictions/results. Do not scale this pair loss or start D3 without a new, justified conditional-signal hypothesis. Official U remains unopened; all Novel-dev results are exploratory.
