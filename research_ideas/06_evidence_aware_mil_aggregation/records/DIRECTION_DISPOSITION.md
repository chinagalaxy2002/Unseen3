# Direction 06 disposition

**Disposition: insufficient evidence.** The frozen one-seed/two-fold comparison of bag BCE (A0) against bag BCE plus reviewed-negative full-query candidate BCE (A2) did not improve pooled inner Novel AUROC. Mean A2−A0 change was −0.45 pp: −0.87 pp on A1 (video-cluster paired 95% CI [−2.97,+1.17] pp) and −0.02 pp on C1 ([-0.93,+0.88] pp). The screen does not support promoting negative-local candidate supervision.

A1 same-query PairAcc increased 0.382→0.449 over 178 pairs, with paired connected-component bootstrap interval [0,+0.072], but C1 changed 0.507→0.498 over 213 pairs, with interval [−0.214,0]. Same-video source PairAcc was unchanged in both folds. Query-only AUROC exceeded either candidate model on both folds (0.671 vs 0.643/0.635 on A1; 0.583 vs 0.549/0.549 on C1). Pre-fusion shuffled-video re-forwarding retained much of pooled AUROC (0.623–0.627 A1; 0.535–0.543 C1), so the retained-label shuffle is a dependence diagnostic and the scores leave substantial non-video-conditioned signal. Neither A2 nor max aggregation rescued the pooled score. Seen AUROC changed −0.96 pp on A1 and +0.43 pp on C1; frozen localization was exactly unchanged because its scorer is untouched. Both arms have 232,449 trainable parameters; A2 used about 1.5× training time on these short runs.

The round tested whether reviewed-negative candidate-level full-query suppression improves the generalization of candidate compatibility scores. The A1 conditional result is a fold-specific mechanism clue; the absent pooled benefit and C1 non-replication mean this is not a method success. The candidate scorer and aggregation were implemented and passed artifact, coverage, and true pre-fusion shuffle validation, so the primary negative result is not an execution failure. Remaining limitations are two folds, one seed, one frozen Moment representation, and no trustworthy positive candidate-slot labels or second-level time map. We did not infer absence from missing labels.

Do not expand A2 or add a grouping grid. Continue to priority 07 with an environment-support audit, while preserving this A1 same-query clue as exploratory. Official U was not accessed; no canonical-backbone or three-baseline claim is made.
