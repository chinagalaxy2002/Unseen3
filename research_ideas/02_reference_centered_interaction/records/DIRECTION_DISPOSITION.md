# Direction 02 disposition (screening stage)

**Disposition: insufficient evidence for a cross-semantic method claim; retain as mechanism evidence and continue in priority order.** The tested method is a rank-32 bilinear scorer on strict-inner masked-mean video/text features. It is not a result for the three canonical GMR scorers or a frozen candidate.

The one-seed, two-fold pilot compared raw BCE-trained B0, fixed-checkpoint post-hoc centered B1, and centered-score BCE-trained B2. Both used 5 epochs, Seen-val AUROC selection, a uniform reference mean over unique inner-train videos and normalized exact queries, and 106,497 parameters. B2 Novel pooled AUROC was 0.6811 vs B0 0.6798 on A1 action (+0.13 pp), and 0.5478 vs 0.5427 on C1 composition (+0.50 pp). Paired video-cluster 95% intervals for B2−B0 were [-0.88,+1.07] pp and [-0.33,+1.33] pp; both include zero and neither reaches the frozen ~2 pp development screen.

Same-query cross-video PairAcc rose from 0.708 to 0.753 on A1 and 0.493 to 0.535 on C1. Connected query-video component bootstrap intervals were [0.000,+0.274] and [0.000,+0.045], respectively; the first is very broad, so the point estimate is not precise. Same-video PairAcc changed 0.734→0.727 and 0.553→0.539. The positive-negative AUC decomposition shows B2 increased within-query PairAcc and cross-query AUC slightly on both folds, but the natural pooled gain remained small. B1 post-hoc alone did not reproduce the A1 conditional improvement. This supports a possible interaction-learning mechanism, not robust mitigation of the primary pooled degradation.

Query-only pooled AUROC was 0.637 / 0.559; video-only was 0.502 / 0.523. B2 retained-label AUROC after an input-level whole-video derangement was 0.541 / 0.522, compared with unshuffled 0.681 / 0.548. These shuffled values are dependence diagnostics with original labels, not valid shuffled-label accuracy. The row-cached masked-mean feature for repeated identical text has small nonzero numerical variation; empirical query-only same-query PairAcc (0.640 / 0.488) is therefore not a valid semantic conditional baseline. See `QUERY_FEATURE_QC.json`; future controls should use one deterministic feature per exact text. Seen AUROC was B0/B1/B2 0.6774/0.6815/0.6849 and 0.6128/0.6140/0.6176. Frozen Seen localization R@1 IoU .5 was 0.348 / 0.350, unchanged by the scorer.

The experiment used strict inner folds whose manifests report zero held-out semantic exposure and zero train/evaluation video overlap. Novel-dev was inspected only after each checkpoint was selected; it is exploratory development data, not formal U. Crossed-quartet metrics were not computed because this pilot used the bilinear scorer and the needed complete trusted matrix audit has not been certified.

Engineering record: the first task IDs exposed a float64 masked-mean query feature and were retained as failed/blocked after two retries. The dtype fix was submitted under `repair1` task IDs. A second generic queue validator issue counted two named raw-scoring arms as duplicate test samples; both raw checkpoints were reused under `eval_reuse1` IDs after changing sample coverage validation to unique `(role,qid,vid,label)` keys. The queue recorded all failures and successful checkpoint reuse. No method, batch size, label, training budget, or selection rule changed between the frozen pilot and its `repair1` training tasks.
