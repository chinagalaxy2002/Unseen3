# Semantic-novel existence screening synthesis

Scope: exploratory strict-inner development only. Across these screens, the official U test split was never opened. The target remains natural pooled AUROC(U+ vs U−); exact same-query cross-video and same-video source-linked rankings are separate conditional diagnostics. A retained-label video shuffle only diagnoses input dependence after fresh pre-fusion re-forwarding; it is not a newly labeled benchmark.

| Priority | Tested mechanism / disposition | What the screen says |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Exact-query cross-video margin fallback — `insufficient_evidence` | A1 pooled and same-query ranking regressed; C1 was near null. This does not test a closed crossed quartet. |
| 02 | Rank-32 reference-centered interaction — `insufficient_evidence` | Small pooled changes with intervals across zero; modest conditional signal is exploratory and cache-sensitive. |
| 03 | Explicit complete-event binding — `blocked` | Reviewed absences exist, but the conservative independent witness challenge is too small and has no validated substitute. |
| 04 | Query-marginal balancing — `insufficient_evidence` | A1 pooled gain tracked cross-query ordering, while same-query PairAcc fell and query-only AUROC was stronger; C1 was near null. Treat the A1 gain as prior-driven. |
| 05 | Independent raw-sequence compatibility adapter — `insufficient_evidence` | A1 same-query ranking improved in one arm but cross-query and same-video rankings weakened; C1 did not confirm a reliable pooled advantage; model capacity differed. |
| 06 | Reviewed-negative local candidate supervision — `insufficient_evidence` | Mean pooled change −0.45 pp; same-query A1 signal did not replicate in C1, same-video ranking was unchanged, and query-only was stronger on both. |
| 07 | Same-video source-pair ERM gate before DG — `insufficient_evidence` | B1−B0 pooled change averaged +0.86 pp with both video-cluster intervals crossing zero. Exact same-query effects pointed in opposite directions; same-video PairAcc did not improve. MLDG/IRM/Group-DRO were not run. |
| 08 | Evidence intervention — `blocked` | No validated time mapping, raw source video, all-occurrence witness audit, or transformed keep/drop/background labels. |

The most consistent explanation supported by current evidence is that pooled scores mix video-conditioned evidence with query-dependent score offsets and construction/semantic priors. Query-only controls often predict above chance, and balanced-query training can raise pooled AUROC without improving within-query compatibility. Fresh shuffling indicates that some arms retain a video dependence signal, but the dependence does not translate into consistent same-query and same-video gains across folds. These observations identify a plausible contributor, not a complete causal explanation of all three canonical GMR baselines. Most intervention runs here use a frozen Moment strict-inner representation/readout and one seed.

The bounded query-prior correction follow-up on Idea 05 is now complete. A1 selected λ=.25 on Seen validation: Seen AUROC rose .8826→.9004, but Novel-dev fell .6335→.5952 (−3.83 pp; paired video-cluster 95% CI −6.87 to −0.71 pp), widening the descriptive Seen−Novel gap .249→.305. Same-query PairAcc was exactly invariant as expected for a query-constant subtraction; same-video source PairAcc fell .643→.462 (paired video-cluster delta CI −.250 to −.106). C1 selected λ=0 and was unchanged. Query-only AUROC remained .648/.572; video-only AUROC .506/.514. Fresh pre-interaction raw-video shuffle was rerun. Thus this correction does not repair the A1 pooled Novel result and harms a conditional same-video comparison; it is not a compatibility gain. Details and full fold-level metrics are in [Idea 05 disposition](../05_independent_compatibility_adapter/records/DIRECTION_DISPOSITION.md), [correction results](../05_independent_compatibility_adapter/records/SCORE_CORRECTION_RESULTS.json), and [per-fold run outputs](../05_independent_compatibility_adapter/runs/score_correction/).

No candidate meets the evidence bar for formal U evaluation or a cross-backbone claim. The evidence supports query-side priors and cross-query score comparability as plausible contributors, but the Seen-selected scalar subtraction screen rejects this particular calibration rule. No further Idea05 variant is justified by the current folds. A next candidate would need independent, predeclared evidence and multi-fold/multi-seed replication before formal U evaluation; do not present the current screens as having solved the degradation across all three canonical GMR baselines.
